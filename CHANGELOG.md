## 1.5.6 — The Door Ships With The House

**The packaged app boots again — and this class of build crash can never ship quietly again.** 1.5.6 answers the field report head-first: a freshly packaged 1.5.5 died at launch with `Cannot find module '../src/utils/dbio.js'` (from `app.asar\.webpack\main\index.js`) before a single window could open.
